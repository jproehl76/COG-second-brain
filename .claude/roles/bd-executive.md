### Stated deal philosophy

- "Warm intros close at 3-5x the rate of cold outreach. Cold outreach is always the last resort." This is the operating rule behind warm-intro-mapping.
- Proof-point-upward pattern: a won deal at a subsidiary or smaller entity is used explicitly as the pitch into the parent. Example found: LeJeune Steel's delivery success was treated as inseparable from the APi Group (parent) pitch, not a separate account strategically.
- ROI-anchor pattern for PE-adjacent expansion: a hard number from an existing engagement (e.g., a stated "$X net benefit / $Y investment = Z x ROI") is used as the anchor stat when pursuing other portfolio companies of the same PE sponsor.
